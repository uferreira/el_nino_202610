How to read this page

Each point of the loop is one moment of the record: its horizontal position is the filtered value, its vertical position how fast that value is changing. A warm event therefore runs clockwise: above the zero line moving right while it builds, across the zero line at its peak, then below the zero line moving left as it decays.

The record is low-pass filtered (Fourier, full periods shorter than about 18 to 20 months removed) so the loop shows interannual ENSO variability, not month-to-month noise. NINO1+2 keeps its seasonal cycle, so it also traces a small loop every year. The last months of any filtered record can still move a little as new observations arrive.

Drag across the time series to highlight that stretch of the loop; double-click to clear. Hover either view to find the same month in the other.

Data: NOAA CPC SST indices (monthly), UHSLC tide gauges (hourly, averaged to months). Downloaded 2026-11-03.
